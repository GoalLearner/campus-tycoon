# Credits

Campus Tycoon uses the art below. This file is written by `tools/build_art.py` from the art recipes.

## Isometric Landscape, Isometric City and Isometric Buildings

- **By:** Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl)
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Zero (CC0) (http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **From:** https://kenney.nl/assets
- **Changes made:** Tiles are layered into whole buildings, quads and lots, then packed into one atlas. Lawn tiles are brightened.
- **Where:** Built into this repository. Every build can fall back to it.

## Nature Kit, Furniture Kit and Platformer Kit

- **By:** Kenney Vleugels (www.kenney.nl)
- **Licence:** Creative Commons Zero (CC0) (http://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/)
- **From:** https://kenney.nl/assets
- **Changes made:** Trees, bushes, flowers and benches are shrunk to the map's scale. Leaves are recoloured from teal to green, and one tree to blossom pink. Soft shadows are added.
