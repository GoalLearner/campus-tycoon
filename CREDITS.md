## Isometric City Builder Pack, Giant NPC Sprite Pack, Medieval City Builder Pack, and Sport and Games Pack

- **By:** Graphics created by Penzilla Design (penzilla.itch.io)
- **Licence:** Penzilla Design Standard Licence: commercial use in one project, credit required, the files may not be shared (https://penzilla.itch.io/)
- **From:** https://penzilla.itch.io/
- **Changes made:** Sprites are shrunk to the map scale and packed into one atlas. The drawn rim on the top edges of grass tiles is painted the colour of the grass, so tiles join. The lawn is a brightened grass tile. The gym is the plain hall with a dumbbell icon on its sign. The outdoor court is cut from the tennis stadium. The first-aid icon is the pharmacy sign. Stadium tiers two and three add a second deck and a second stand made from the stand the stadium already has. Red crosses on the medical building and the first-aid icon are recoloured green. Road pieces and cars are mirrored to face every way. The crossing is a road piece with its own lane marks repeated across it.
- **Where:** Used in builds that are given the private art pack.
